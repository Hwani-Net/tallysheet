2026 E-Commerce Fee FAQs

What fees are included in Etsy US vs. Etsy KR/Global?

Etsy US models $0.20 listing + 6.5% transaction + 3.0% + $0.25 payment processing. Global/Korea Payoneer payouts add higher cross-border processing and ~2% FX spread buffer.

How is the Reverse Sale Price calculated?

We solve closed-form pricing algebra: (Hard Costs + Fixed Fees) / (1 - Variable Fee % - Target Margin %), automatically handling Etsy's $100 Offsite Ads cap when exceeded.

Why does Shopify Basic + 3rd-Party PG show an extra 2%?

In regions without Shopify Payments (including South Korea), Shopify adds a 2% third-party transaction fee on the Basic plan on top of your external gateway fee.
